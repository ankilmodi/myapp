## 🎯 **How to Use Entry Price:**

### **What is Entry Price?**
- **Entry Price** = Best price to BUY the stock
- Set **0.5% below Current Price** (small dip)
- Gives better entry than buying at market price

### **Example:**
```
Stock: SAIL
Current Price: ₹120.50
Entry Price: ₹119.90 (buy at or below this)

Wait for price to come to ₹119.90 or below
Then BUY!
```
